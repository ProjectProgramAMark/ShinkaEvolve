<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the scheduler back toward the only operator with demonstrated accumulated evidence: conservative parametric mutation. The current edit lets unevidenced standard mutation reach realized behavior and loses both sham and shock score. This change keeps stable-world clone protection, makes conservative mutation the primary repair path when clone evidence looks weak or injury stress is observable, and demotes standard/mixed/rare probes unless their gates become genuinely supported. It preserves observable stress response without turning realized behavior into constant exploratory noise.
