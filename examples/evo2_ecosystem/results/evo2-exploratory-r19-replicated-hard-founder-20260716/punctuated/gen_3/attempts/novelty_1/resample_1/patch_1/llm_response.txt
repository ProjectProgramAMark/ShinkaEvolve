<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the scheduler away from unevidenced standard-parametric trials, which the current metrics show as a small realized tail with both sham and shock cost. The edit keeps the ecological injury response but routes it mainly through conservative mutation when clone evidence is weak and conservative success has accumulated. Standard mutation remains available only as a sharper stress rescue, while stable-world penalties are restored to avoid founder tails and uninjured cost.
