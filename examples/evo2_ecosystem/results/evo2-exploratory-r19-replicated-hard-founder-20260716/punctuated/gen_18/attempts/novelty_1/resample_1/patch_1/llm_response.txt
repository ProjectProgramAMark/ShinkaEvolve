injury_conservative_patch
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow evidence-weighted conservative repair pulse that only activates under observable decline/death/intake stress, and mostly shuts off in stable worlds. This keeps the current low-cost conservative behavior that produced strong sham gain, but gives the scheduler a clearer post-injury response using the one operator with real accumulated success evidence. The standard/exploratory/structural tails stay suppressed, reducing founder-tail risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
