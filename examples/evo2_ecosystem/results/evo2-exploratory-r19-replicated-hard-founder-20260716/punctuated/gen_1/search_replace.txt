injury_weighted_conservative_repair
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the policy head away from low-evidence standard mutation and toward conservative repair when observable stress, clone underperformance, and accumulated conservative success agree. The edit keeps stable conditions clone-heavy, but lets proven conservative mutations expand under injury pressure. Standard, exploratory, structural, and mixed choices remain tightly bounded and require stress plus operator evidence or unused-trial pressure, reducing stable-world cost and tail risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
