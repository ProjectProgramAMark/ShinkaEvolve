injury_probe_credit
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded injury-only probe credit for standard parametric mutation when population decline/death and intake or energy gaps are visible, while suppressing it in stable worlds. This gives the scheduler a small route to gather standard-mutation evidence after injury even when prior evidence is absent, without raising stable-world mutation pressure.
</DESCRIPTION>
