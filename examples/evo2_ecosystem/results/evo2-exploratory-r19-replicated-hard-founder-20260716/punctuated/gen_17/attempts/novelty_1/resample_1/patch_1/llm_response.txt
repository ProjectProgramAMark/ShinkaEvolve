conservative_rescue_ladder
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the scheduler from broad standard-parametric exploration back toward an evidence-ratcheted repair ladder. The current program pays stable-world cost by giving standard mutation a high base logit, while observed evidence still only supports conservative mutation. This edit lowers the standard baseline, restores conservative mutation as the first injury/stress response, and keeps standard mutation available mainly through existing injury-gated repair/probe terms when clone performance is poor. Exploratory, structural, and mixed remain rare and stress-gated to avoid founder tails.
</DESCRIPTION>
