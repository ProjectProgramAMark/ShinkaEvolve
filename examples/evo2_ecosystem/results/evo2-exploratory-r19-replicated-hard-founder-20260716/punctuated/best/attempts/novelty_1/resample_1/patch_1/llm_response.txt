stress_weighted_repair
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the small mutation budget away from quiet conditions and toward observable population stress. The current scheduler already finds the conservative band, but pays stable-world cost while giving too little stress-only repair. This edit raises conservative repair only when ecological stress and clone underperformance are visible, adds a bounded standard trial path when clone evidence is poor, and strengthens the stable lock so quiet worlds stay closer to clone behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
