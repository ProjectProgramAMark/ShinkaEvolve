<DESCRIPTION>
Add a small evidence-weighted conservative rescue credit that activates only when observable stress/injury is present and conservative mutation has accumulated a success margin over cloning. This shifts injury-world choices toward the already-beneficial conservative operator without raising exploratory or structural tails, while the stable lock suppresses the credit in uninjured worlds.
