<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow standard-parametric rescue window that only opens under observable ecological stress, when standard mutation is unused and plausibly better than cloning. This should raise non-clone exploration after injury while keeping stable-world behavior close to the frozen-clone baseline. The change is intentionally bounded and evidence-aware: it uses the correct operator_stats row ABI, boosts standard only when decline/death/intake-energy stress are present, and damps the window in stable or low-alive founder-tail states.
</DESCRIPTION>
