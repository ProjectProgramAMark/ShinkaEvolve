<DESCRIPTION>
Adds a small standard-parametric repair probe that only rises under observable injury/stress, reproductive readiness, low stable-world signal, clone underperformance, and conservative evidence. It also slightly lowers clone pressure and raises standard pressure only through that probe, aiming to recover injury benefit while keeping ordinary-world mutation cost low.
</DESCRIPTION>
