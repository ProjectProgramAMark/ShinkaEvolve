<DESCRIPTION>
Use the observed evidence pattern more conservatively: clone has substantial evidence and mediocre success, conservative has low but better success, while standard has essentially no evidence. The edit shifts injury/stress rescue pressure first into conservative parametric mutation and makes standard mutation earn its way in through either accumulated evidence or clear conservative fatigue. This should preserve the small non-clone benefit after injury while reducing stable-world and founder-tail cost from premature standard trials.
