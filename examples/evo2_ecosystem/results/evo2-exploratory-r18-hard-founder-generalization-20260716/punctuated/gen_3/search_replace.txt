<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the zero-evidence standard-mutation prior back toward a cautious rescue-only role, and make stress probing depend on actual clone weakness plus reproductive readiness. The current program gives standard mutation a small stress-probe subsidy even with no standard evidence, but the observed run still selects almost no standard mutation while paying a slightly worse paired delta. This edit keeps the stable-world clone bias, modestly restores the conservative channel as the safer learned alternative, and prevents broad fixed standard exploration unless stress, clone underperformance, and exploitation capacity coincide.
</DESCRIPTION>
