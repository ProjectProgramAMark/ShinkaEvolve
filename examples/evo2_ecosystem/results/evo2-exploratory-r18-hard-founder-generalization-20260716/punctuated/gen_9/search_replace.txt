stress_release_probe

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow stress-release gate that only activates when ecological stress is high, stable-world no-spend pressure is low, the parent has enough reproductive leverage, and clone evidence is accumulating as weak. This keeps sham worlds clone-heavy, but gives standard and conservative parametric mutation a real chance during injury recovery instead of leaving the policy effectively unconditional clone. The release is still bounded by novelty, fatigue, and fragile-parent penalties, so it should avoid broad fixed mutation and founder-tail exposure.
</DESCRIPTION>
