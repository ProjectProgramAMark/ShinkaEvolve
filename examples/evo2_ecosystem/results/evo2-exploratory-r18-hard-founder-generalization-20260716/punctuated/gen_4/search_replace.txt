stress_only_standard_probe
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow, ecology-conditioned standard-mutation probe that activates only under stress with reproduction capacity and low stable-world signal. The current scheduler almost never samples standard mutation, so it cannot recover the known injury upside. This keeps stable-world clone protection intact while giving the system a small path to collect evidence and exploit standard mutation when decline/death/intake stress indicate injury-like conditions.
</DESCRIPTION>
