<DESCRIPTION>
Add back a narrow standard-mutation rescue pulse, but keep it gated by ecological stress, reproduction readiness, unused/evidence opportunity, and anti-stable-world pressure. The current program almost never selects standard mutation, so it loses the injury benefit while still paying a small stable-world mutation cost through conservative mutation. This change gives standard mutation a chance only when the ecology is deteriorating and clone evidence looks weak, while leaving stable worlds clone-heavy.
