<DESCRIPTION>
Make conservative mutation pay a larger evidence tax in stable renewal worlds, while preserving stress-triggered standard rescue credit. The current run still spends about 1.5% on conservative mutation with very low conservative evidence, and the observed cost is mostly sham-side. This change separates "clone is underperforming" from "conservative mutation is proven enough to use in stable conditions": clone weakness can still open injury/stress rescue, but stable-world conservative drift now needs accumulated conservative evidence and success. Standard rescue also regains a narrow stress-probe term so injury worlds can exploit unused standard mutation without raising broad stable mutation.
</DESCRIPTION>
