<DESCRIPTION>
Separate genuine injury-rescue opportunity from ordinary stable renewal. The current scheduler still spends about 1.5% on conservative mutation and pays a sham cost while standard mutation is effectively never selected. This edit adds a compact rescue-specific gate that requires stress, injury signal, low stable-world confidence, and reproductive readiness. Stable worlds get a stronger clone lock and less conservative renewal pressure; standard mutation receives a narrow rescue-only pulse so the policy can keep injury upside without broad fixed mutation.
</DESCRIPTION>
