<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the broad scalar recovery boost with a scarcity-weighted, ecology-gated probe. The current scheduler adds recovery pressure to rare mutation operators even without requiring underuse, missing evidence, or a clear non-calm rescue window. This edit makes mutation exploration conditional on pressure exceeding calm conditions, suppresses it under death-heavy fragility, and weights it by operator uncertainty so clone/conservative behavior remains dominant unless the ecology and evidence justify trying non-clone heredity.
</DESCRIPTION>
