recoverable_evidence_window
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded transfer-oriented mutation window that only opens under recoverable ecological stress: viable population, non-collapsing survival, meaningful normalized need, and a parent with above-local energy/intake. This should raise conservative and standard parametric use where mutation has trusted evidence, while suppressing founder-specific over-triggering during calm growth or severe decline. The mechanism remains ecology-conditioned and evidence-adaptive, without using identity, seed, time, or hidden partitions.
