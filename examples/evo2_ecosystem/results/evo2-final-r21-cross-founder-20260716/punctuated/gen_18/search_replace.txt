trusted_pulse_standard_mixed
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow trusted-innovation pulse that only activates when normalized ecological pressure, parent-relative fitness, viability, and observed mutation trust align. This keeps clone dominant in calm or weak-evidence founder states, but shifts a small amount of probability from clone/conservative toward standard and mixed mutation when the ecosystem is stressed and operator evidence supports mutation. The mixed/structural channels remain strongly gated, avoiding the earlier founder-specific over-triggering failure mode.
