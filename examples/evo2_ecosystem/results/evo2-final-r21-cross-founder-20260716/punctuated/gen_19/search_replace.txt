<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the broad recovery probe with a stress-excess gate that only lowers clone and boosts mutation when ecology shows normalized rescue pressure beyond calm baseline. This keeps the mechanism ecology-conditioned, but reduces founder-specific over-triggering in healthy founders. It also adds a trusted relative-advantage term against clone, gated by rescue ecology, so operator evidence can help transfer without rewarding unevidenced exploratory/structural operators everywhere.
</DESCRIPTION>
