trusted_tempered_pressure
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Temper ecology pressure with population viability so injury/founder stress does not automatically unlock broad mutation, while still preserving a real ecology-conditioned response. Add a normalized trusted-evidence gate that favors conservative and standard parametric mutation only when mutation operators have evidence-backed success relative to clone, and when the parent is locally better than the population. This should reduce founder-specific over-triggering while keeping non-clone adaptation alive.
</DESCRIPTION>
