trusted_normalized_pressure
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Use founder-normalized ecological pressure instead of raw stress, then gate mutation credit through operator evidence relative to clone trust. This keeps ecology-conditioned non-clone behavior, but reduces transfer failure from scarce or untrusted operators being rewarded just because they are unused. The logits are also softened toward conservative and standard parametric mutation while suppressing exploratory, structural, and mixed mutation unless ecology and evidence both support them.
</DESCRIPTION>
