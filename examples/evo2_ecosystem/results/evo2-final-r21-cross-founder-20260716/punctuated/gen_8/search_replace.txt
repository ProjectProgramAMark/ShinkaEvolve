trusted_recovery_probe
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a bounded recovery-probe gate that raises exploratory, structural, and mixed mutation only when ecology is favorable: parent quality is decent, compact genomes have room to vary, pressure is not extreme, and population/birth signals suggest useful adaptation. It also adds an evidence-centered adjustment so trusted operators can gain share while weakly evidenced operators stay mostly exploratory rather than dominating. This keeps the scheduler ecology-conditioned and non-constant, but avoids the sealed-founder failure mode of broad mutation over-triggering.
