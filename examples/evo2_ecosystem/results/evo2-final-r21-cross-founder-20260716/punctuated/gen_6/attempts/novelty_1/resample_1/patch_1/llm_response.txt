<trusted_parametric_relief>
A targeted boost for conservative and standard parametric mutation when two transferable signals agree: normalized ecological need is present, and observed parametric evidence is beating clone evidence. This keeps clone dominant in calm/low-need regimes, avoids founder identity or event timing, and gives the scheduler a little more room to exploit the only operators with measured success above clone instead of leaving mutation nearly absent.
