<DESCRIPTION>
This edit makes mutation pressure depend more on ecology-normalized distress and cross-operator trust, instead of subtracting clone trust directly. The current relative-trust term appears to have made the policy more clone-heavy and reduced both sham and shock deltas. The replacement adds a bounded injury-like rescue gate from intake deficit, decline/death pressure, low growth, and birth scarcity, then uses evidence-weighted non-clone advantage only when operators have real evidence. It also applies a calm/growth penalty to poorly evidenced operators so exploratory, structural, and mixed mutations do not over-trigger on sealed founders. The mechanism remains ecology-conditioned and non-constant, with small but genuine high-pressure scout boosts.
