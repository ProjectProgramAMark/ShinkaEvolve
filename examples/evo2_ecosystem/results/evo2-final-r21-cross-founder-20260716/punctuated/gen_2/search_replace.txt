normalized_trust_gate
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Use ecology-normalized stress with a reproductive deficit gate and require parent advantage plus trusted mutation evidence before opening mutation logits. This keeps a real ecology-conditioned scheduler, but reduces founder-specific over-triggering by making mutation pressure depend on normalized population need and actual trusted operator evidence rather than raw stress alone.
</DESCRIPTION>
