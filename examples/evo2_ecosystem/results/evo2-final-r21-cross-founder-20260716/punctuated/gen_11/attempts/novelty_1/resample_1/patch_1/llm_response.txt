<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded operator-evidence escape signal when clone has substantial evidence and low success, but gate it through normalized ecological pressure and mutation trust. This keeps the scheduler ecology-conditioned and evidence-adaptive while shifting only modestly toward conservative and standard parametric mutation, the two operators with observed success. Exploratory, structural, and mixed remain strongly suppressed unless their own evidence improves.
</DESCRIPTION>
