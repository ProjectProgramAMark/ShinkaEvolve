<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow standard-mutation probe that only opens under observable ecological stress: decline/death pressure, intake or energy shortfall, reproductive readiness, and low stable-world signal. The current scheduler improves sham but still almost never samples standard mutation, so it cannot retain the known injury benefit. This edit gives standard mutation a small evidence-seeking path after injury while adding explicit clone pressure in stable worlds and low-vigor tail states to limit sham cost and founder-tail failures.
