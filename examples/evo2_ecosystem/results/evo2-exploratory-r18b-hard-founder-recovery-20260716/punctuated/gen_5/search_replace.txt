<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow standard-mutation pulse that only opens when the ecology shows observable injury-like stress: falling population, elevated death, intake or energy deficit, and enough reproductive capacity to absorb mutation. The pulse is explicitly suppressed by stable_world/stable_lock and low_alive, so it should avoid paying the stable-world sham cost that previous broader standard boosts incurred. It also depends on unused standard-operator capacity plus clone underperformance evidence, so it adapts from accumulated operator summaries instead of hard-coding a fixed non-clone rate.
