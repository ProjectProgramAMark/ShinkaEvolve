injury_limited_standard_probe

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow standard-mutation trial path that only opens when the observable ecology looks injured rather than merely stable: decline/death stress, noncollapsed alive fraction, viable parent vigor, reproduction readiness, and unused/low-evidence standard operator history. It is explicitly suppressed by stable_world and fragile_lock to avoid sham-world cost and founder-tail failures. The pulse then gives standard mutation a readable rescue boost from this gate while slightly taxing clone only in the same gated injury context.
