buffered_rescue_probe
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The current recovery probe admits standard mutation in marginal worlds and lowered survival/sham performance. This edit keeps the injury-rescue idea but makes standard mutation conditional on a buffered ecology: enough alive population, non-collapsed birth flow, and credible standard evidence or a large clone deficit. It also reduces the direct standard pulse from this probe and returns some pressure to clone when the ecology is not clearly buffered. The intent is to preserve rare post-injury standard trials while avoiding stable-world cost and founder-tail failures.
</DESCRIPTION>
