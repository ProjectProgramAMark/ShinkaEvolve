injury_standard_trial_gate
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded injury-only standard mutation trial gate that treats low standard evidence as uncertainty worth probing only under ecological stress. This should recover some persistent-injury benefit by lifting standard parametric mutation when populations are declining or intake-limited, while adding an explicit learned penalty when standard mutation has accumulated bad evidence. Stable and fragile regimes still push back through stable_caution and fragile_signal, so this does not simply raise mutation everywhere.
</DESCRIPTION>
