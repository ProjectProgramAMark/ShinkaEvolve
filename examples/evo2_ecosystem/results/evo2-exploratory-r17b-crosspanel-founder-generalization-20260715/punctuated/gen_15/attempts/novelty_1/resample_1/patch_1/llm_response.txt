clone_pressure_rescue_release
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow evidence-conditioned rescue release: when clone has strong evidence but poor success, and the ecology is stressed without looking stable or fragile, give standard parametric mutation a small extra path. This preserves the current clone-heavy stable behavior while allowing injury worlds to escape exact-clone inertia using accumulated operator evidence instead of unconditional mutation. Stable and fragile brakes are slightly tightened to protect worst-founder tails.
</DESCRIPTION>
