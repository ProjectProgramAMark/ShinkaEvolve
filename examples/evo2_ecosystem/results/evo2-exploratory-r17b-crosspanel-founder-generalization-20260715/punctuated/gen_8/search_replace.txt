<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrower rescue window that only spends mutation budget when ecological stress looks injury-like, the parent is viable, and the founder/world is not already fragile. This shifts a small amount of probability from clone into standard and conservative mutation during persistent injury conditions, while strengthening the stable-world brake so sham regimes keep the clone-heavy behavior that was already working.
