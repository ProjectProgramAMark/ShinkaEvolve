evidence_gated_rescue_window
</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add an injury-specific rescue window and operator badness brake so mutation is concentrated when ecological stress suggests head-actuator injury recovery, while stable or fragile founders revert closer to clone. This keeps the standard mutation benefit in shock worlds but cuts broad conservative drift that appears to be hurting the paired aggregate. The edit uses the existing success, usage, and evidence vectors in the required row-major ABI.
