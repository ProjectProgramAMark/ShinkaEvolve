<DESCRIPTION>
Add an evidence-weighted fatigue signal for over-used conservative mutation, convert it into a stable-world mutation debt, and use that debt to suppress conservative drift where sham/stable founders are fragile. At the same time, add a narrow standard-mutation rescue credit gated by injury stress and low prior standard usage, preserving the observed shock-world benefit without broadly increasing mutation. This targets the current failure mode: conservative mutation is being selected too often despite weak success, harming sham aggregate, while standard mutation remains useful mainly under injury stress.
