fatigue_gated_conservative

<DESCRIPTION>
This edit adds an evidence-based fatigue term for conservative mutation when it is already heavily used but not succeeding enough. The current scheduler improved shock worlds but pays a sham/stable penalty by selecting conservative mutation about a third of the time even after its measured success falls. The change suppresses conservative renewal mainly in stable non-injury regimes, shifts that mass back to clone, and preserves standard mutation under ecological injury/stress where the current program is gaining. This is not unconditional cloning: the brake depends on conservative usage, evidence, and success, while the standard rescue path remains stress/injury gated.
</DESCRIPTION>
