<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrow standard-mutation rescue lane that is only active under genuine ecological stress, while adding an explicit stable/sham suppressor. The current scheduler already gets most of its gain from shock worlds, so this edit avoids broad mutation increases: standard gets extra support only when injury_rescue, stress_core, clone underperformance, and unused/low-evidence standard opportunity align. In stable or fragile regimes, the same signal suppresses standard and mixed mutation and nudges clone/conservative back up to reduce negative tails.
